## When is Home Depot's Black Friday?

The in-store event starts at 6 a.m. the day after Thanksgiving. Home Depot's corporate holiday schedule has stores closed on Thanksgiving Day and opening at 6 a.m. on Black Friday — a pattern that's held consistently, with deal trackers confirming the same arrangement for 2026, including a 9 p.m. close that Friday evening.

Online runs ahead of the stores. Home Depot typically launches pre-Black Friday deals weeks before Thanksgiving, and online doorbusters generally go live before doors open — which matters, because the doorbuster items are the ones that sell out. Deal-site guidance for Home Depot specifically recommends creating your online account before the sale starts for exactly this reason.

One naming quirk worth clearing up: Home Depot also runs a **Spring Black Friday** every April. That's a separate, smaller event built around lawn and garden — mulch, soil, plants, trimmers — with discounts reaching 70% off in those categories. If you found this article in spring, that was the sale you were reading about. The rest of this piece covers the November event.

## What actually goes on sale

Holiday decor, power tools, appliances, and smart home gadgets carry the deepest cuts. Deal trackers covering the Home Depot Black Friday ad expect discounts up to 45% off plus BOGO offers across brands like RYOBI, DEWALT, Milwaukee, Samsung, LG, GE, Husky, Shark, Ring, Google, and Cuisinart, with free delivery on most orders. TopCashback's own overview of the sale puts it higher — up to 60% off site-wide on Christmas trees, holiday decor, power tools, appliances, and tech — so the honest range is "up to roughly half off, with seasonal decor at the deepest end."

A realistic caveat on those percentages: the "up to" figure applies to holiday items. Lumber, plumbing, and electrical supplies barely move during Black Friday because their margins are already thin. If your list is a new fridge, a drill combo kit, and a twelve-foot skeleton, you're shopping the right sale. If it's renovation materials, the sale won't help much.

Big appliances get extra treatment beyond the sticker discount — special financing offers and delivery deals are typical, and Home Depot's credit card frequently adds promotional financing on qualifying purchases (6-month financing on $299+ is a common baseline offer outside the sale).

Also useful to know before you go: Home Depot runs a 10% military discount for active duty, veterans, and spouses, with some exclusions. There's no student or senior discount.

## What stacks and what doesn't

The layering rules at Home Depot are stricter than at many retailers, and Black Friday tightens them further:

- **Price matching is suspended for Black Friday.** Home Depot has a Low Price Guarantee on regular purchases, but sale prices during Black Friday are explicitly excluded from price matching. Don't plan to price-match a competitor's ad during the sale.
- **Coupon stacking generally isn't allowed**, Black Friday included — though gift cards can be used as payment.
